# @angular-community/qwik-cdk/a11y

Qwik port of [@angular/cdk/a11y](https://github.com/angular/components/tree/main/src/cdk/a11y) (focused subset for Dialog).
Exports `_IdGenerator` (stable cross-SSR IDs), `InteractivityChecker`, `InputModalityDetector`, `FocusMonitor`, `FocusTrap`, and the `useFocusTrap` / `useFocusOrigin` hooks with their context IDs and `use*` helpers. `useFocusOrigin()` is the lazy (`useOn()`-based, no per-instance `useVisibleTask$`) hook consumed by `qwik-material/button-toggle` and `qwik-material/button`; `list` is an expected future candidate. `FocusMonitor`/`createFocusMonitor`/`FOCUS_MONITOR` remain exported as the faithful low-level CDK port (programmatic `focusVia()`, or a shared engine wired up manually) — see SYNC.md for why the old `useFocusMonitor()` convenience hook was removed in favor of `useFocusOrigin()`.
The `LiveAnnouncer`, `cdkAriaLive` directive, `cdkTrapFocus` directive, and `FocusKeyManager`/`ActiveDescendantKeyManager` are deferred. See SYNC.md for porting deviations.
